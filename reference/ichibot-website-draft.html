Questions we hear often

Something else on your mind? Talk to our engineers directly.

Do we need to replace our existing machines?

No. Ichibot works with the PLCs, meters and machines you already have, and adds retrofit sensors where data isn't available yet.

Where is our data stored?

Your choice. Data can stay fully on-premise, go to the cloud, or both. The edge device always keeps a local log.

How long does a typical project take?

A pilot on one line usually goes live in 2–6 weeks after the site survey, depending on the number of devices and integrations.

Can it integrate with our ERP?

Yes, through API, direct database connection or MQTT. We map the data together with your IT team during implementation.

What happens when the internet goes down?

The edge device keeps collecting and logging data locally, and syncs automatically once the connection is back.

Tell us about your plant

Our engineers will visit, map your machines and data, and propose a pilot that fits your budget.
